## 3. Why we are changing (say it in your own words)

1. **The next 3 months are about proving that Kivu Ride works, and collecting every franc.**
2. **The numbers:**
   - each managed car must bring **360,000 a week**;
   - **240,000** goes to the owner, **120,000** is ours;
   - our goal is to keep **98% of it every week**.
3. **30 new electric cars arrive in December.** What we fix now is what lets us grow with them.
4. **One rule for everyone: if it is not recorded in Kivu Daily, it did not happen.**
   - Calls, payments, drivers, decisions and tasks are all recorded there.
   - Nobody enters anything about a driver unless it was done by us or approved by Finance or the MD.
